Rate shifts (skyline, piecewise-constant FBD) in ffbd (ffbd_sky.cpp, 14 Nov 2026; now also ffbd.cpp): checks. Usage: results/skyline_usage.md.
Model: K shift ages (input rate_shifts T1 ... TK, or --rate-shifts) give K+1 intervals with their own lambda_i, mu_i, psi_i; rho at the present,
r = 0, sampled ancestors, root or origin conditioning as before; density of RevBayes 1.3.2 dnBDSTP(..., timeline=v(T1,...,TK)), rates youngest
first. Each free rate ~ Exp(10). Optional shift_rates (--shift-rates) chooses which of lambda, mu, psi vary; the others are shared.
Moves: scale per free rate (weight 3 each), up-down per interval (weight 2 each), each tuned separately; the sampled-ancestor fossil-age move
becomes a Metropolis-Hastings step when shifts are present.

1. Regression (no shifts = previous binary, byte for byte). 3M proposals, seed 7, .log and .trees compared with cmp.
   examples/example_input.txt, example_crown_stem.txt, bears_input.txt x {default clock, --burnin 1e5, --no-data, --clock gam, --clock acln,
   --mode control}: 18/18 identical on the laptop (clang) and 18/18 on OSCER (g++ 13.3 -O3 static, job 36057843; reference = ffbd_release.cpp).
   Also identical: GS_Jn --clock gam 3M; stepping-stone .pp and .ml.tsv (--ss 8, example); bears igr 2M with checkpoints, .ckp file included.
   ffbd_sim without shifts: identical input and truth line for seeds 11-13, root and origin. make test (release folder, submission-prep): all tests passed.
   (One fix was needed: on Apple clang the up-down move's prior term must keep the old expression, or fused multiply-add changes the
   last digit of cached sums; it now does.)

2. FBD log density against RevBayes 1.3.2 dnBDSTP (scripts/sky_density_check.py; jobs 36056781, 36056976, 36057214-36057217, 36057842).
   1,012 state evaluations. Each configuration has 20 MCMC states (--no-data, rates and tree sampled) plus 16 states at random fixed rates
   (log-uniform 0.005-1 per rate and interval, --fix-fbd).
   RevBayes computes ln N! of the tree-shape term by Stirling's series to the 1/(360 N^3) term. That gives a constant offset of
   -4.6519e-8 at N = 7 (it matches the observed offset to 5e-14 in every state), -1.5e-10 at N = 22 and below 1e-12 for larger trees.
   The "corrected" difference removes this constant; raw differences are that constant plus the corrected one.

      RevBayes agrees with the 50-digit value on the same three trees to 1.5e-9; with 66.0 it reproduces 1.4e-5, 1.8e-5, 2.0e-6 (job 36057842).
      So the deviation is on the RevBayes side, for ages at a shift time. In MCMC such ages have probability zero (fossil ages are continuous).
   b) 1 degenerate state (gs 23,66,252, state 27): a non-SA fossil (Saurorhamphus judeaensis) with an age exactly equal to its parent node,

      tree, and the constant-rate moves can reach it the same way. 1 of 1,012 states; not a skyline issue. Possible fix, not made here because
      it would change the constant-rate code: reject a proposal that makes a non-SA fossil's age equal to its parent's.

   the states at shift times. The degenerate state is the only exception (0.68; the mp script reads both zero-length fossil tips as SAs).

   rates of intervals spanned by the tree are not Exp(10). Two checks therefore:
   a) the FBD density switched off (test-only flag --no-fbd): every rate of every interval = Exp(10).

      example, lambda varies, mu and psi shared: 5 rates, KS p 0.26-0.96.
   b) FBD density on, an interval older than the root (shifts 7,14,40, root < 25): its rates are not in the density and recover Exp(10):
